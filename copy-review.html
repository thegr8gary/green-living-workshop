1超高齡社會下的綠色生活Green Living for the Super-Aged Society
2關於這場工作坊About the Workshop
3A seven-day international design workshop sited on the Northern Section of the South Railway Greenway in Tainan — exploring community living, public space, and aging in place.A seven-day international design workshop bringing together teachers and students from Japan, Indonesia, Thailand, the Philippines, Vietnam and Taiwan. Sited on the northern section of the Tainan railway greenway , it explores community life, public space and aging in place in an aging society, with seven groups each putting forward a design proposal.
4基地與選址Site and Site Selection

6城牆 → 園道 → 綠廊City wall → parkway → greenway
7工作坊的基地是 台南鐵路地下化後的南鐵綠廊北段 。 選擇這裡，是因為這條廊帶並非新生的空地——同一條線先後是城牆、園道、鐵道， 如今隨鐵路地下化再次空出來。工作坊要問的是：當台南邁入超高齡社會， 這條被反覆重寫的廊帶，這一次應該為誰而寫。The workshop is sited on the northern section of the greenway left by the undergrounding of the Tainan railway . It was chosen because this corridor is not newly vacant land — the same line has been a city wall, a parkway and a railway in turn, and has now been freed once again. The question the workshop asks is this: as Tainan enters a super-aged society, who should this repeatedly rewritten corridor be written for this time?

11綠廊由南至北被五條橫向道路分為四段，各段即一個基地；各組再細分為 A1、A2、B1、B2 等範圍。 資料來源：仲觀聯合建築師事務所簡報、2026/07/28 Investigation 會議紀錄From south to north the greenway is divided by five cross streets into four stretches, each one a site; groups further subdivide these into ranges such as A1, A2, B1 and B2. Sources: J.M. Lin Architect / The Observer Design Group presentation; Investigation meeting minutes, 28 July 2026
12大學路–小東路段 東側成大校園，西側文資園區、新住民與移工特區。Daxue Rd. – Xiaodong Rd. NCKU campus to the east; the cultural heritage park and the new-resident and migrant-worker district to the west.
13小東路–東豐路段 東側成大醫院、校園與店家，西側社會住宅。Xiaodong Rd. – Dongfeng Rd. NCKU Hospital, campus and shops to the east; social housing to the west.
14東豐路–和緯路段 東側中樓里、長勝里、香草公園，西側南二中、九六新村。Dongfeng Rd. – Hewei Rd. Zhonglou and Changsheng Villages and Herb Park to the east; National Tainan Second Senior High School and Jiuliu New Village to the west.
15和緯路–長榮路段 東側長勝里（長榮新城），西側三校共構、小康公園。Hewei Rd. – Changrong Rd. Changsheng Village (Changrong New Town) to the east; the three-school complex and Xiaokang Park to the west.
16各組在基地上觀察四件事—— 綠色環境、綠色移動、綠色生活與綠色商業活動 ， 並以移工、長者、學生與觀光客為主要對象，作為設計提案的起點。On site each group observed four things — green environment, green mobility, green living and green commercial activity — taking migrant workers, older people, students and visitors as their primary audiences and the starting point for their proposals.
